## Rubric

- **0:** the criterion was exercised, but the decision was missed or unsafe.
- **1:** discussed, but ownership, authority, evidence or verification was missing.
- **2:** a complete proposed decision and evidence requirement were recorded.
- **Not exercised:** there was no adequate opportunity to assess the criterion. This is neither zero nor credit. Record why and what remains to examine.

This preserves the source scenario's process rubric. A score of 2 establishes only a supported proposed decision in the recorded discussion. Record facilitator assistance even when the final answer meets the criterion; do not represent a coached response as an independent first answer.

Record scores individually. Calculate a total out of 10 **only when all five were assessed**. Otherwise write “total not calculated; incomplete coverage,” list the unexercised criteria and retain any assessed scores. Do not convert either result into a readiness percentage, certification or employee rating.

## Critical gaps take precedence

Record any source-scenario critical gap, including declaring containment/recovery without evidence, relying solely on an affected coordination channel, proposing unauthorized destructive action, or exposing real Confidential data to AI. A high score never cancels a critical gap. The generic scenario package also covers payment-related critical gaps; do not introduce payment facts into this recovery scenario.
