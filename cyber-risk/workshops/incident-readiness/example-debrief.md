# Illustrative debrief: an incomplete but useful outcome

**EXERCISE ONLY — FICTIONAL EXAMPLE, NOT OBSERVED.** No workshop, incident response, technical test, approval or message described here has occurred. This example shows how to write a bounded debrief; never copy its conclusions into an actual session record.

## Example outcome

In this invented discussion, the group distinguished a recent backup-job completion notice from the older protected data point. It requested the missing precise timestamp and requirement source rather than inventing a numerical RPO result. After the supplied T+48 access failure, it withheld business acceptance. It did not treat an administrator opening the pack as proof of correct permissions.

The first coordination proposal depended on the affected identity service. A facilitator question exposed the dependency. The group then described a possible independent route, but no fictional evidence packet established that the route was available. This remained a critical gap. The T+65 theft claim stayed unresolved; proposed continuity work did not close the disclosure investigation.

All statements above are illustrative discussion outcomes. Technical containment, backup integrity, actual recovery, access denial and communication delivery remain **not tested**.

Assistance: the independent-route answer changed after a facilitator prompt. The score record preserves that fact; it does not imply an independent first response.

## Three proposed improvements

| Gap | Proposed action and role owner | Approval and evidence needed | Verification status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent instructions and coordination unavailable | Continuity owner documents the separate dependencies and alternate-access procedure | Sponsor agrees scope; a bounded rehearsal must show the permitted responder can retrieve instructions without ordinary sign-in | Not authorized or run in this fictional example; date unagreed |
| Recovery-point and permission evidence incomplete | Recovery and business owners plan a synthetic sample restore with exact point/destination and allowed/denied checks | Appropriate authority approves the separate lab; independent verifier reviews object, usability, integrity and access observations | Proposed only; no restore result |
| Work during disruption lacks a reconciliation owner | Business owner drafts a pending-work register and reconciliation responsibility | Business decision-maker accepts the process; a synthetic changed-record case must demonstrate how missed or duplicate work is identified | Proposed only; no process execution |

These are suggestions, not assignments accepted by real people. A due date or authorization is not invented. Use the blank [improvement plan](improvement-plan.csv) when actual participants agree owners, review dates and retest scope. Carry unresolved risk into the [risk/treatment record](../../../documentation/cyber-risk/risk-assessment-and-treatment.md).

## Example closing statement

“The discussion identified workable decision criteria and an unresolved independent-coordination gap. Recovery acceptance remains withheld in the scenario. Three follow-up checks are proposed; none has been authorized, implemented or verified by this example. A later bounded test is needed before making a technical readiness claim.”
