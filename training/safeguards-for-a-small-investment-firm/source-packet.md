# An investor asks to see the program

You are helping Quillon Ridge Partners, a fictional venture firm of fourteen staff and one contractor, answer a prospective investor's questionnaire about its safeguards program. Everyone and everything in this packet is fictional: the firm, the investor, the vendors, and every name and domain. The exercise reference date is 21 September 2026. D1 describes what the firm actually has today and is deliberately thin. D2 is the investor's questionnaire excerpt. D3 lists the roles you can assign work to.

Policy: one document, 'Quillon Ridge Information Security Policy', two pages, dated March 2021, written by the former office manager. It says staff must use strong passwords, lock their screens, and 'report anything suspicious to a partner'. It names no owner and has never been revised.

## D2: Due-diligence questionnaire excerpt

Date: 2026-09-16 · Section 7, Information Security, from the operational due-diligence questionnaire sent by Osprey Meadow Family Office (fictional)

7.1 Provide your written information security program and identify the individual responsible for it by name and title.

7.2 Describe your most recent information security risk assessment: its date, who performed it, and how findings were tracked to closure.

7.3 Describe access controls over investor and portfolio information, including multi-factor authentication coverage and how access is reviewed and removed when staff leave.

7.4 Describe how investor information is encrypted at rest and in transit, naming the systems that hold it.

7.5 Describe how you assess third-party service providers before engagement and monitor them afterward, including your fund administrator and IT provider.

7.6 Provide your incident response plan. State how, and within what period, you would notify us of an incident affecting our information.

7.7 Describe security training for staff, its frequency, and how attendance is recorded.

7.8 Describe how the program is monitored and tested, and give the date and scope of the most recent test.

7.9 State when the program was last reviewed, by whom, and what changed. Responses are due 16 October 2026.
