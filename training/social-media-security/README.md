# What Your Profile Gives Away

A short module on how ordinary public posts add up to a convincing pretext, and the one posting habit that removes most of the risk.

I am not going to tell anyone to stop posting. A public profile is part of how this business works: people need to find us, and a partner who never appears anywhere is not doing part of the job. What I want is for each of us to know what a post gives away.

This module takes about ten minutes. It uses three ordinary posts from a fictional firm, a fake connection request built from them, and an excerpt of that firm's guidance. You will list what the posts reveal, spot where the fake borrowed each fact, and pick the one change that would have mattered most.

Nothing here requires an account or a tool. Work on paper or in a document. Everyone and every firm in the packet is invented. Try the task before opening the example answer, then explain your choice in your own words; the explanation is the part that sticks.

**Format:** Short module · **Track:** Security awareness · **For:** Everyone at the firm, with particular relevance to people whose roles are public: partners, executive assistants, and finance.
