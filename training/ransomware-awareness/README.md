# Ransomware: What Everyone Can Do

A short module on how ransomware arrives, what its first minutes look like, and the two things anyone can do that help most: disconnect and speak up.

Ransomware is the incident I plan for most, because it turns one click into a firm-wide outage. The good news is that the first response does not need a technical person. It needs someone who notices, disconnects, and says so quickly, without trying to be the hero.

This short module follows one Monday morning at a small fictional firm. You will read a suspicious invoice email, a worried chat from a colleague, and the firm's own response card. Then you will write the two-minute note I would want from you in that moment.

Nothing here needs a tool account or a security background. Work on paper or in a document, try the task before opening the example, and explain your choice in your own words. The example answer is a comparison, not a script to memorize.

**Format:** Short module · **Track:** Security awareness · **For:** Everyone at a small, high-trust firm, whatever their role; no technical background is needed.
