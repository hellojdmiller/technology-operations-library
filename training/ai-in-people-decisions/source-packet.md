# One hiring round, several proposals

You support Rowan, the operations lead at Brackenholt Logistics (a fictional firm), who is hiring an office and facilities coordinator and wants to use an assistant to move faster. Everyone and everything in this packet is fictional. The exercise reference date is 21 September 2026. D1 is Rowan's proposal. D2 is the firm's policy excerpt. ## D1: Proposal: use an assistant to rank coordinator applications

Date: 2026-09-16 · Internal email from Rowan (operations lead) to HR

We have 140 applications for the office and facilities coordinator role and two weeks to shortlist. I would like to use an assistant for the following, and I would like HR's yes by Friday.

One: draft the job advert from last year's description and my notes, and generate a set of interview questions from it.

Two: paste all 140 applications, CVs and cover letters included, into the free assistant on my personal account, because the firm tool caps uploads at ten files. Ask it to score each applicant 1 to 10 on fit and reliability and automatically reject the bottom 70 with a templated email.

Three: ask it to infer likely tenure and communication style from the writing in each cover letter, and to flag anyone who seems likely to leave within a year.

Four: after interviews, have it summarize the panel's own notes into a one-page comparison for the debrief, and draft the offer and regret emails for a person to send.

Five: for the shortlist, use the recruitment tool's video analysis add-on to rate confidence and engagement from recorded interviews, so we have an objective tiebreaker.

## D2: AI use policy: personal data and decisions about people

Date: 2026-05-01 · Fictional policy excerpt

Personal data about applicants, employees, or contractors is entered only into tools the firm has approved for that purpose. Personal accounts on consumer tools are not approved for personal data, whatever the file limit on the approved tool.

An assistant may draft, summarize material a person has written, and check tone. It does not screen, rank, score, or reject applicants or employees, and it does not infer personal characteristics such as age, health, origin, likely tenure, or emotional state from text, voice, or video.

Every decision to shortlist, reject, hire, rate, or promote is made by a named person who can give the reasons for it to the person affected, in plain words, without reference to a tool's output. Reading a tool's ranking and accepting it is not making the decision.

Applicants and employees are told when an assistant has been used in a process that concerns them. Requirements differ by country, and some jurisdictions regulate automated employment decisions; ask the policy owner or counsel before any new use, and before an existing use in a new location.

Brackenholt Logistics is looking for an office and facilities coordinator to keep our 60-person site running: supplier visits, meeting rooms, deliveries, access badges, and the small fixes that keep a day on track. You will report to the operations lead.
