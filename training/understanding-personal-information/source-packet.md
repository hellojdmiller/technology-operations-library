## D3: Personal data handling rules (excerpt)

Date: 2026-07-01 · Fictional policy excerpt: collection, locations, requests, and exposures

Collect only what a task needs, keep it only while it is needed, and review holdings once a year. Data with no current purpose is deleted under the retention schedule, not kept in case.

Personal information about clients, investors, employees, or candidates lives only in approved locations: the managed shared drive, the CRM, the HR system, and firm mailboxes. It does not go to personal email, personal cloud storage, messaging apps, removable drives, or any AI assistant or online tool without a written firm agreement covering that data.

When a task needs part of a file, share an extract with only the columns and rows required, through the approved location, rather than the whole file.

Requests from a person to see, correct, or delete their information go to the privacy lead, Dana Whitlock, on the day they arrive. Do not answer them yourself. Deadlines and rights depend on where the person lives, and Dana decides.

Suspected exposure, including a file sent to the wrong place, a lost device, or unexpected access, is reported to Dana and IT the same day. Do not investigate alone, do not delete evidence, and do not contact the affected people before Dana has assessed it.
