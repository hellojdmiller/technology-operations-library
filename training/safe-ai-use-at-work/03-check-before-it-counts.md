# 3. Check before it counts

Trace every figure, name, date, and quote to the source before the draft goes anywhere.

Suggested time: 15–20 minutes. Work on paper or in a document.

**Outcome:** Produce a corrected draft with each correction traced to the packet.

Read the [shared fictional packet](source-packet.md). Keep it available throughout the lesson.

Generative tools produce confident text. NIST calls the failure mode confabulation: erroneous or false content stated with confidence, even in its citations. The danger is not obvious nonsense; it is the plausible number in the right place, or the quote given to the wrong person. [S3](sources.md#s3)

So I trace figures, names, dates, and quotes to a line in the source. A claim I cannot find is removed or marked, never 'probably fine'. NIST suggests fact-checking generated information and verifying the citations in outputs. [S3](sources.md#s3)

Microsoft says of its product that responses are not guaranteed factual and users should review output before sending it to others. The assistant drafts; a person decides and signs, and says the draft was AI-assisted where a reader would expect it, as D2 requires. [S4](sources.md#s4)

## Worked example: A number that reads right and is not in the source

**Before**

The board has agreed in principle to proceed at the revised indicative price of 4.3 million, a 5 percent increase on the July figure.

**After**

The board has not yet decided whether to proceed at the revised indicative price of 4.1 million (D1, paragraph 2). No earlier figure is in the packet; the July comparison goes.

**Why:** Two facts changed and one disappeared. Each change points to a line in D1, and the missing comparison is stated as missing rather than kept. A reader can check each word against the source in a minute.

No. A citation is a claim about where something came from, and NIST notes that generated citations can themselves be confabulated. The check is the same: open the source and find the sentence. A tidy citation on a wrong claim only makes it look checked. [S3]
