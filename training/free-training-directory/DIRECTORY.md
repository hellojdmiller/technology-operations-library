### 1. Adobe Learn

[Open learning site](<https://www.adobe.com/learn>) · **Public learning** · Adobe

**For:** Communications staff, Marketing staff, Document owners.

**Free scope:** Free Adobe tutorials and courses spanning design, photography, video, and document work.

**Access and limits:** Adobe Learn publicly advertises free learning. Sign-in supports a saved learning journey; paid Adobe applications or premium features are not supplied by the tutorials. A software trial is not counted as free training entitlement.

**Suggested starter task:** Using a fictional PDF, write a redaction verification checklist that checks visible content, search results, and copied text. Practice only in an authorized app with the required features, or review the steps on paper.

**Evidence reviewed 2026-09-19:**

- [Adobe Learn](<https://www.adobe.com/learn>). Publication/update: unknown. Supports: Explicitly offers thousands of free tutorials and courses and includes PDF redaction, protection, and editing topics.
