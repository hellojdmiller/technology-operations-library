## D3: Offboarding checklist and how to raise a concern (excerpt)

Date: 2026-05-12 · Fictional policy excerpt: the leaver process and the concern route

Leavers. The manager submits the leaver form at least five working days before the last day. Accounts are disabled by 18:00 on the last day. Laptop sync is switched off earlier where the technology lead judges it sensible.

Contractors. Every contractor account carries an end date set when it is created, and the identity system disables it on that date. Extensions need a new end date from the manager. The technology lead reviews all contractor accounts quarterly.

Work files. Files on shared drives belong to the firm and stay there. Personal copies of firm files are not permitted, including for a portfolio. A leaver who needs specific files asks their manager, who decides what can be released and how.

Raise a concern. If something about access or data does not look right, take it to the technology lead, Ines Duarte, first. Frame it as a question: 'I noticed this, is it expected?' Nobody is criticized for a good-faith question, and you do not need to be sure.
