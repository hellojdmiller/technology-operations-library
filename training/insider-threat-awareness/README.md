# Insider Risk Without Suspicion

A short module on why most insider incidents are mistakes or convenience, what the rare deliberate ones look like, and how to raise a concern without accusing anyone.

Insider risk is an awkward subject at a small firm, because the insiders are people you like. I want to take the suspicion out of it. Almost every incident I have handled was a shortcut or a gap in process, and the fix was a better process, not a better interrogation.

This short module follows one week at a small fictional firm. You will read a departing colleague's request, an access report with one line that does not add up, and the firm's offboarding checklist. Then you will write the kind of note that gets a problem fixed without making anyone a suspect.

Nothing here needs a tool account or a security background. Work on paper or in a document, try the task before opening the example, and explain your choice in your own words. If you manage people or contractors, the third paragraph of the lesson speaks to you directly.

**Format:** Short module · **Track:** Security awareness · **For:** Everyone at a small, high-trust firm, with a short note for anyone who manages people or contractors.
