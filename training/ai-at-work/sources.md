## S3

**[How AI assistance impacts the formation of coding skills](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-assistance-coding-skills)**

Publisher: Anthropic (Judy Hanwen Shen and Alex Tamkin)

Published or updated: 2026-01-29

Reviewed: 2026-09-19

**Supports:** In a randomized study of 52 mostly junior Python developers learning Trio, the AI group averaged 50% on an immediate quiz versus 67% without AI; the completion-time difference was not statistically significant.

**Our application:** Ask learners to make an attempt, explain a correction, and distinguish task output from understanding.

**Limits:** Small coding study with an immediate assessment. Interaction patterns were qualitative associations, not randomized teaching methods. It does not establish lasting skill loss, results for everyday writing, or effectiveness of this course.

**Revisit when:** Revisit after relevant replications, longer follow-up, or evidence from comparable employee writing tasks.

## S4

**[Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232635/)**

Publisher: Bastani and colleagues, Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences

Published or updated: 2025-06-25 (online publication)

Reviewed: 2026-09-19

**Supports:** Assisted practice improved in both AI conditions. Ordinary answer-oriented assistance harmed later unassisted exam performance; the teacher-informed tutor largely avoided that harm without showing an unassisted advantage over the control.

**Our application:** Offer a first attempt, an optional hint, an answer explanation, and a changed-case question; keep assisted and independent observations distinct.

**Limits:** One high school in Turkey, mathematics, a Fall 2023 deployment, and short-term outcomes. This was not employee training or a test of static feedback. It does not demonstrate this course's learning effect.

**Revisit when:** Revisit when studies test comparable workplace tasks, longer-term outcomes, or different assistance designs.
