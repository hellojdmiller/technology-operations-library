# 3. Check it before you share it

Repair a plausible draft and explain the corrections.

Suggested time: 15–20 minutes. Work on paper or in a document; an AI account is optional.

**Outcome:** Prepare an update and action list; distinguish drafting, approval, and sending.

Read the [shared fictional packet](source-packet.md). Keep it available throughout the lesson.

A smooth draft can be wrong. I check details that change what someone does: arrival time, room, remote access, and commitments. Tone comes after accuracy.

Check D4 against D1–D3, not its confidence or another assistant's agreement. Use the relevant notes, message, or policy. [S1](sources.md#s1)

I ask for an explanation before showing the answer. Studies involving coding and school mathematics distinguish task completion from understanding. They did not test this course. Our approach is to try first, compare the answer, and explain one correction in your own words. [S3](sources.md#s3), [S4](sources.md#s4)

## Worked example: Correct the claim, not just the wording

**Before**

Refreshments are approved, so everything is ready.

**After**

Refreshments are awaiting the budget owner's written approval. Noor will request the decision; no decision deadline has been agreed.

**Why:** This preserves the open decision and follow-up owner without inventing a deadline.
