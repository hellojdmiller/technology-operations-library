## D4: How to report a suspicious message

Date: 2026-09-01 · Firm instructions: one page from the technology team

Report first, ask second. In your mail client, select the message and choose Report, then Report phishing. If the button is missing, forward the message to phishing@harbourline.example.com. For a text or a call, send a screenshot or a short description to the same address. Reporting takes ten seconds and lets us check every other inbox.

Never reply, never click, never open the attachment, and never call a number the message gives you. That includes unsubscribe links and 'verify your account' buttons.

Verify through a channel the message did not supply: the internal directory, a colleague in person, or a website you type yourself. Our only email domain is harbourline.example.com. Look at the address after the @ sign and the registered domain in any link, not the display name.

Payments and account changes: any new or changed payee, urgent payment, or request to keep a payment quiet is confirmed by voice with the requester on a directory number and approved by Dana Whitfield. A text or an email never authorizes a payment, whoever it appears to come from.

Shared-document notices and calendar invites: open documents from the portal you type yourself, not from the notice. Do not accept or decline an unexpected invite; report it and we will remove it.

If you clicked, opened, replied, or entered a password: tell the technology team immediately at techhelp@harbourline.example.com, on extension 220, or Leo Marsh in person. Out of hours, call the Technology on-call number in the directory. Do not delete the message. Do not shut down, wipe, or unplug anything. Do not change your password until we tell you which device to use. If you cannot reach anyone within 30 minutes out of hours, change your password from a different trusted device and tell us what you did.

No blame. Nobody spots every message; a late report is the only thing that makes an incident worse. We will never penalise a report, including a report of your own click.

Our own security notices come from security-alerts@harbourline.example.com, contain no links, and direct you to the contacts above.
