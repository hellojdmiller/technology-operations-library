## D3: Email guidance (excerpt)

Date: 2026-06-15 · Fictional policy excerpt: tagging, reporting, forwarding, rules, and sending

Every message from outside the firm carries an [EXTERNAL] tag in the subject. A colleague's name on an external message is a reason to check the address, not a reason to trust it.

Suspicious messages are reported with the Report button in the mail client, which sends the message to IT and the mail provider for analysis. Do not delete, reply, or forward them to colleagues for opinions. If a message asks to change payment details, confirm by phone using the number already in the firm directory.

Work mail is not forwarded to personal accounts, by hand or by rule, and automatic forwarding outside the firm is blocked where the system allows. Check your own rules and forwarding settings monthly.

Any rule or forward you did not create is reported to IT the same day as a possible account takeover. Leave it in place for IT to examine, and change your password when IT confirms the mailbox is clean.

Before you reply or forward: check who is on the To and Cc lines, especially after auto-complete; use Reply All only when everyone needs the answer; and trim earlier messages from a thread before forwarding it outside the team.
