## D3: Excerpt: the firm's rule on phone requests

Date: 2026-06-01 · Fictional policy excerpt

No one at Fennimore Grove Advisors, and no bank, vendor, or service provider we work with, will ask you for a one-time code, a password, or remote access to your computer by phone. If a caller asks for any of those, end the call and report it the same day.

Payment approvals and changes to payment details are never accepted by phone, voicemail, or text, including from a partner. They go through the payments queue with the usual two approvers, whatever the deadline. A caller who asks you to skip that step is asking you to break the rule.

If you need to confirm something a caller said, hang up and call back using the number in the firm directory, on the back of the card, or on a previous statement. Do not use a number the caller gives you, and do not trust the name or number shown on caller ID.

Report suspicious calls and voicemails to it-help@fennimoregrove.example.com the same day, with the time, the number shown, and what the caller asked for. Reporting a call that turns out to be genuine is fine and costs nobody anything.
