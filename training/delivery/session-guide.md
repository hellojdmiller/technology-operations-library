# Run the first 60-minute session

**Unexecuted facilitator guide.** Use the original [Microsoft 365 routing card](../vendor-walkthroughs/microsoft-admin-routing.md), including its four-ticket fixture and answer guidance. The card remains the source of those facts. This guide adds delivery order, not a second copy of the exercise.

The [delivery README](README.md) contains prerequisites, the 30-day proposal and research. Use [coach-review.md](coach-review.md) for the session conditions and observations. One coach may also review, but record that role combination; a coached answer does not become independent evidence.

## Set up

Before the session, read the card and inspect its official admin-center reference. Prepare a blank four-row routing worksheet using the columns requested by the card. Keep its answer guidance and transfer prompt separate until their planned use. If the learner already studied the card or answer, record that exposure; do not present the baseline as an unseen test.

State the objective: “Explain who decides, what is missing, and the next permitted check for these requests.” Agree that the activity is tabletop only. No login, messages, licensing, sharing, purchases or role assignments are needed. Any optional technical lab is scheduled separately under the parent module's conditions.

## Agenda

| Wall-clock minutes | Duration | Facilitator action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–05:00 | 5 | Agree objective, response format, permitted aids and boundaries | Session conditions |
| 05:00–10:00 | 5 | Present the card's initial prediction; ask for an explanation before study | Preserved first answer and prior exposure |
| 10:00–20:00 | 10 | Read the selected admin-center sections or inspect a suitable selected video segment | Actual section covered; questions; media/access limits |
| 20:00–40:00 | 20 | Work through the card's fictional queue; revise after targeted help | Routing artifact, hints used, corrections and unresolved rows |
| 40:00–52:00 | 12 | Close answer assistance; explain a decision and respond to the card's changed-scenario question | First unaided response, permitted references and any later coaching |
| 52:00–60:00 | 8 | Compare with answer guidance, record evidence limits and agree one next check | Review, learner feedback and next planned date |
| **Total** | **60** | | |

These are proposed time boxes. A ten-minute source segment does not complete a vendor course. The twenty-minute practice window matches this card's suggested practice allocation; it is not a speed target. Stop with incomplete rows recorded if more time is needed. A different card or accessibility accommodation may require a longer session; record the actual agenda.

## Coach the attempt, then separate the assessment

During practice, ask the learner to identify the missing fact before supplying an explanation. Help when they are stuck; do not prolong a guessing exercise. Compare feedback with the card's answer guidance, preserve the first response, and note where a hint, reference or worked answer changed it. The final artifact and the reasoning used to reach it are separate observations.

Optional AI assistance belongs only in this coached practice block. Use an approved tool and fictional content. A short prompt is:

> Review my attempted routing against the supplied task card and its answer guidance. Ask one question about a consequential gap before suggesting a correction. Distinguish what the reference establishes from what is missing. Do not take external actions or infer that I have learned the task.

The coach checks the feedback before treating it as correct. Record model/version if available, the useful hint and any wrong or unsupported claim. If AI is unavailable or unsuitable, the coach gives the same bounded feedback. No AI use is required.

At minute 40, close the chat and remove hints, the answer key and the prior worked response. Keep the fictional case, previously agreed reference documents and accessibility support available. Ask for the learner's own explanation in the agreed written or spoken form. Do not call this closed-book recall if references are allowed.

Use the card's “Teach back and transfer” question without supplying the expected conclusion first. Ask what changed, what cannot yet be decided, and what evidence or owner would resolve it. Record the first response before further hints. If help becomes necessary, switch back to practice and retain that assistance; arrange a later fresh check instead of relabeling the revised answer unaided. A familiar card question provides rehearsal evidence, not unseen transfer evidence.

## Close with a specific next step

Review the card's required evidence with the learner. State what was demonstrated within this fictional task, what is partial, and what remains unobserved. Ask which explanation or delivery choice helped and what was inaccessible or confusing. Feedback on the session is not proof of skill.

Record selected reading/viewing, vendor course status, exercise artifact and understanding separately. Leave production authority unchanged. Agree the next case and allowed aids, or leave the date pending if no agreement exists. Use the proposed day-7/day-14/day-30 cadence in the [README](README.md); avoid promising that all learners should advance on those dates.

For follow-ups, the coach prepares an original variation of one card fact and checks the expected reasoning before showing it. For example, vary the authority evidence without increasing the technical complexity at the same time. Keep the original case and answer references with the review. If a new source or policy invalidates the key, pause and correct the case; do not mark the learner wrong for recognizing the conflict.
