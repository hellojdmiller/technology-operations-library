# Working Securely Anywhere

Three practical lessons on keeping a laptop or phone trustworthy, working on someone else's network without fear, and handling personal devices and unapproved apps.

Most of us now work from a kitchen table, a train, a hotel desk, or a phone in a queue. That is normal and it is fine. I want the device in your hand to be trustworthy wherever it is, and I want you to know the first call to make when it is not.

This course follows one fictional firm through one ordinary week: a laptop left in an airport lounge, a colleague who wants a personal note-taking app for client meetings, and a hotel Wi-Fi page that asks for a little too much. You will check a device, plan a trip, and answer a request.

No technical background is needed and no tool account is required. Work on paper or in a document, try each task before opening the example answer, then explain your reasoning in your own words. The suggested 15–20 minutes per lesson is a starting estimate, not a limit.

**Track:** Security awareness · **For:** Everyone at a small, high-trust firm who works from home, travels, or uses more than one device, with no technical background assumed.
