BEFORE THE RUN
[ ] Define the task, quality criteria, consequence, and authorized scope.
[ ] Record model/alias/version, prompts, tools, permissions, and source set.
[ ] Record service availability and comparison differences; distinguish
    a hardware claim from the accessible service and measured task result.
[ ] Define context/memory strategy, source authority, and correction path.
[ ] Define the baseline, measurement coverage, and follow-up window.
[ ] Explain any learning/transfer exercise to participants in advance.

FIVE ACCEPTANCE CHECKS — STATUS AND EVIDENCE REQUIRED
[ ] QUALITY: Requirements met; material errors and uncertainty visible.
[ ] AUTHORITY: Owner and approvals match this use and these actions.
[ ] PATH: Observed data, tools, recipients, and actions stayed in scope.
[ ] EVIDENCE: Claims and effects have inspectable support; gaps stay visible.
[ ] FINAL STATE: A verifier checked the intended artifact or destination.

RECOVERY AND MEASUREMENT
[ ] Identify stop/containment owner, affected state, recovery steps,
    irreversible effects, notification authority, and reopening checks.
[ ] Count preparation, production/prompting, supervision, review,
    correction, and recovery effort.
[ ] Keep machine wait separate; include rejected/blocked/abandoned attempts.
[ ] Count a task once after successful retries; retain every attempt's cost.
[ ] Mark missing measurements unknown; zero requires an observation.
[ ] Record delayed defects and understanding separately; missed follow-up
    does not mean zero defects or retained understanding.
