# Managing a technology vendor through its lifecycle

A vendor review should answer whether the service fits the intended work, what the firm will depend on, and how it can leave. Keep those questions alive after purchase.

## Before a trial

Record the business problem, existing alternatives, sponsor, users, data, integrations, permissions, expected cost, and decision deadline. Use the [software approval procedure](../documentation/procedures/software-request-and-approval.md) to produce a bounded trial with a measurable success condition.

Review evidence for the actual service, account tier, and contract. A generic security page may not describe the product or region being purchased. Identify missing evidence and assign a reviewer instead of treating an unanswered questionnaire as complete.

## Before production

Confirm the owner, account and administrator controls, permissions, logging, data handling, support route, deployment process, and exit method. Review provider dependencies and service limits. Establish how access is removed and how data is exported in a usable format.

For AI and integrations, document downstream providers, information scope, actions, retention settings, and changes that trigger reassessment. Approval should name the allowed use, not simply the vendor.

## While the service is operating

Maintain the service record, access reviews, incident history, changes, and support evidence. Revisit the assessment after a material capability change, acquisition, integration expansion, data-scope change, or repeated control failure.

Track both the renewal date and the notice deadline. The deadline for giving notice may determine the actual decision window. Record budget ownership and who is authorized to cancel or negotiate.

## Renewal decision

Compare business value, actual usage, cost, support performance, unresolved risk, and the cost of switching. Produce an explicit recommendation: renew, reduce, renegotiate, replace, or retire. Record the decision, authority, and action completion separately.

The [renewal and license workflows](../n8n/README.md) can prepare candidate reviews from synthetic records. They do not authorize cancellation, reclaim licenses, or infer that an inactive user no longer needs access.

## Exit acceptance

Verify data export and readability, migration reconciliation, access revocation, connector and token removal, billing cancellation, ownership transfer, and available deletion evidence. Record contractual retention or technical limits that remain. Keep required records under the applicable internal process.

Use the [vendor ownership training](../training/README.md) to rehearse the handover with a fictional service before relying on the process.
