# Technology operations field guide

Start with the business capability and its owner, then choose the policy, procedure, configuration review, or automation that supports it. These guides describe proposed operating practices and fictional examples, not a particular firm's environment.

| Guide | Question it helps answer |
|---|---|
| [Operating model](operating-model.md) | Who recommends, approves, executes, and verifies the work? |
| [First 90 days](first-90-days.md) | What should I establish, test, and make repeatable? |
| [Employee lifecycle](employee-lifecycle.md) | How do we provision, change, and remove access with evidence? |
| [Fund and investor operations](fund-and-investor-operations.md) | Where do technology, records, recipients, and business approval meet? |
| [Portfolio support boundaries](portfolio-support-boundaries.md) | What help are we offering, and who owns the resulting decision? |
| [Vendor lifecycle](vendor-lifecycle.md) | How do we evaluate, operate, renew, and exit a service? |
