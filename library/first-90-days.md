# The first 90 days of a VC IT review

This is an illustrative sequence for an incoming IT owner or a firm improving its operating model. Adjust it to urgent risks, staffing, and business deadlines. Calendar milestones are planning aids, not permission to defer a known serious issue.

## Days 1 to 30 Establish facts and ownership

Identify critical business workflows, decision-makers, providers, privileged identities, renewal deadlines, recovery dependencies, and open incidents. Confirm access to the systems you are responsible for through the approved process. Separate what is documented, what someone reports, and what you have verified.

Produce a small [service catalog](templates/service-catalog.csv), an initial [risk register](templates/risk-register.csv), and a list of decisions requiring an owner. Review the [operating model](operating-model.md) with leadership and providers. Preserve existing evidence before restructuring records or changing access.

**Exit evidence:** The firm can identify the owner and support route for each critical capability, name unresolved high-impact questions, and find the relevant operating records.

## Days 31 to 60 Test the important claims

Walk through a joiner, role change, departure, sensitive sharing decision, software request, vendor renewal, and bounded recovery exercise. Use synthetic data and an isolated test environment where practical.

Review the [Google and Microsoft baselines](../baselines/README.md) against the actual edition, licensing, architecture, and evidence. A desired-state control is not an observation about the tenant. Record unassessed and unsupported items clearly.

**Exit evidence:** Critical assumptions have either a test result or a named unresolved dependency. Proposed treatments include ownership, approval, and acceptance checks.

## Days 61 to 90 Make operation repeatable

Agree a sustainable review cadence and assign alternates. Adopt the useful [documentation examples](../documentation/README.md) through the internal approval process. Use [training modules](../training/README.md) to verify that another operator can complete the work and handle a failure.

Introduce [n8n review workflows](../n8n/README.md) where reliable source records and clear decision ownership already exist. Automation should make missing evidence visible; it should not manufacture a clean status from incomplete inputs.

**Exit evidence:** An alternate can operate a defined service, find the evidence, escalate a blocked decision, and explain what remains outside their authority.

## Leadership update format

Report the business capability improved, evidence supporting the result, current risk or limitation, decision needed, owner, and next verification date. Avoid a list of tools purchased or tickets closed without the business consequence.
